Delete your account and data

In the app

  1. Open Hatim Groups.
  2. Tap Edit Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account Permanently and confirm.

Without the app

Email us from your account's address. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest.

cebeci.zafer@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your Shahid profile and display name
  • Your Khatm group memberships
  • Juz assigned to you become free; completed juz stay in the group's progress without your name
  • Groups you created pass to the co-admin, otherwise the oldest member; a group with no members is deleted

What is kept

Your email sign-in record is not deleted because it is shared with other ZaiC Design & Studio apps.
